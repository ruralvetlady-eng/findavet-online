First Visit Checklist

A little prep makes your appointment smoother and more productive. Use this checklist to save time and ensure nothing gets missed.

Bring

  • Medical records, prior lab results, and vaccination history
  • Current medications/supplements and doses
  • Photos/videos of symptoms (limping, coughing, skin issues)
  • Food/treat labels or ingredient lists if discussing diet
  • Carrier/leash, recent photo, microchip number

Share

  • Timeline of symptoms: when they began, what helps or worsens
  • Behavior changes, appetite, energy, bathroom habits
  • Any toxin exposure concerns (chocolate, meds, plants, cleaners)
  • Travel history, boarding, new pets, or environmental changes

Ask

  • Top two or three likely diagnoses and how to confirm them
  • What to monitor at home and red flags to recheck sooner
  • Estimate for today vs. estimate if we wait
  • Whether pet insurance can help with this visit
