Emergency Care Basics

If you suspect an emergency, the safest move is to contact a veterinary hospital immediately. Use this guide to recognize urgent signs and prepare for the visit.

Red-flag symptoms

  • Trouble breathing, blue/gray gums, collapse, seizures
  • Uncontrolled bleeding, deep wounds, severe lameness
  • Known or suspected toxin ingestion
  • Repeated vomiting/diarrhea with lethargy or blood
  • Eye injuries, bloat (distended abdomen, retching), not urinating

On the way

  • Call ahead so the team can prepare
  • Secure pets in a carrier or with a leash/harness
  • Bring meds list and recent records if available
  • Avoid home remedies unless instructed by a veterinarian

At the ER

Expect triage on arrival. The team will stabilize life-threatening issues first, then discuss diagnostics and costs. Ask what’s most urgent vs. what can wait.
