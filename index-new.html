Helpful Extras

Questions to Ask Your Vet

  • What’s the diagnosis and confidence level?
  • Best-case, likely, and worst-case outcomes?
  • Costs today vs. costs if we wait?
  • Home-care steps and red-flag signs?

Bring-Along Checklist

  • ID, microchip #, recent photos
  • Medical records & meds list
  • Food, treats, comfort item
  • Carrier/leash & payment method

When to Seek ER Care

Trouble breathing, collapse, seizures, active bleeding, toxin ingestion, bloat, inability to urinate/defecate, severe pain, or eye injuries.

This site is educational and not a substitute for an in-person exam. If in doubt, call a veterinarian or emergency hospital.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FindAVet a veterinary clinic? ⌄

No. We’re a directory and resource site to help you locate licensed veterinary care and prepare for visits. Always follow your veterinarian’s advice.

Is the information here medical advice? ⌄

This content is educational only and not a substitute for a physical examination or diagnosis. If your pet is unwell or in pain, contact a veterinarian.

Do you list emergency hospitals? ⌄

Yes — our USA and Global pages prioritize emergency resources and official associations when available so you can get help fast.
